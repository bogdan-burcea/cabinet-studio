## Activation gate for each product pack

Before a candidate becomes executable: pin official drawing/document revision and page, region and handedness; list required companions/accessories/fasteners; encode supported stock, clearance, reference faces and deductions; distinguish manufacturer data from process/construction policies; add representative official-drawing fixtures; perform physical trial fitting; record geometry fidelity, known limitations and verification date. Only then allow the assembly to be selected by cabinet intent.
