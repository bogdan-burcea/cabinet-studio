## Rules and presentation

The first supported width range is 240–900 mm clear, an application policy. Each allocation must be at least 98.5 mm high, fit its leaf opening and not overlap another drawer, an adjustable shelf or any slab-door coverage. Clear depth must be at least 503 mm. Both runner mounting faces must be uninterrupted full-depth sides/dividers; recessed mounting dividers fail. Missing openings fail dependent edits without losing intent. Board widths regenerate from the current opening.

Fronts occupy the opening allocation with 2 mm gaps on each edge and sit 2 mm ahead of the carcass; this is the application's first front policy, not a manufacturer overlay recommendation. Front stock follows carcass thickness. Bottom and back are separate 16 mm chipboard components irrespective of cabinet stock. Steel rendering is simplified, explicitly tagged envelope geometry; board dimensions are exact to the cited formulas, but display placement does not define machining. Extension is illustrative, not a certified travel or swept-clearance model.

For the 600 mm cabinet with 18 mm sides: LW=564, bottom=489×476×16, back=477×84×16 mm. Runner rating is manufacturer information for its pair and never a cabinet/load/stability approval.
