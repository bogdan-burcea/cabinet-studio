Keep research references separate from canonical concept records. Before product activation: obtain/inspect the correct regional drawing and revision/page, pin all companions and preparation limits, encode representative calculation fixtures, and record physical trials. A component rating never establishes cabinet, shelf or wall-system capacity. Product reference records and outstanding checks are available locally in the app; cabinet files continue to save their existing concept pins and empty enabled-hardware list.
